# Social asset pack

Generated by `scripts/generate-brand-assets.js` (group `social`) from HTML templates and the SVG logo set. No photographs, no third-party artwork, fonts from `assets/fonts`. Nothing here is uploaded anywhere: you upload each file yourself (the table says where). The hound is the only brand mark; the orange hound is the same drawing as the logo, filled.

Regenerate: `NODE_PATH=<folder with playwright>/node_modules node scripts/generate-brand-assets.js social`
Your own words in the same style (post, story or thumbnail): `node scripts/generate-brand-assets.js make --kind post|story|thumb --headline "..." [--sub "..."] [--light] --out file.png`

## Where the sizes come from (checked 23 Oct 2026)

- X: 400x400 profile photo, 1500x500 header, JPEG, GIF or PNG under 2 MB. Source: X Help Center, "Help with uploading a profile photo" (https://help.x.com/en/managing-your-account/common-issues-when-uploading-profile-photo). The header's lower-left corner is covered by the profile photo (third-party guides).
- LinkedIn: logo 300x300, cover 1128x191, PNG or JPEG up to 8 MB. Sources: third-party guides that quote LinkedIn's table (for example https://create.microsoft.com/en-us/learn/articles/linkedin-image-sizes). I did not find LinkedIn's own help page in the search: confirm in the page editor when you upload.
- YouTube: banner 2560x1440 with a centred safe area of 1546x423, up to 6 MB. Sources: third-party guides (for example https://havecamerawilltravel.com/youtube-banner-size/); YouTube's own page did not come up. The 800x800 icon is YouTube's usual recommendation and shows as a circle; unverified in this check.
- GitHub: social preview 1280x640 (minimum 640x320), PNG, JPG or GIF under 1 MB. Source: GitHub Docs, "Customizing your repository's social media preview" (https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/customizing-your-repositorys-social-media-preview). The organization avatar size (500x500) is a safe square size, not a documented requirement.
- Product Hunt: thumbnail 240x240, gallery images 1270x760 or larger, at least three images recommended. Source: the launch form and guides that mirror it (for example https://screenhance.com/blog/product-hunt-launch-checklist-2026). Check Product Hunt's current submission page before you launch.

## Rules for anything made from these templates

The score measures readiness. Never write that an assistant recommends, cites or names a brand because of Citehound. No outcome promises, no invented numbers, no testimonials. See `docs/brand/BRAND.md` and `docs/integrations/RULES.md`.
