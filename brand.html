Using the logo

Do

  • Put it on a plain flat ground: ink, paper or white.
  • Leave clear space on every side equal to the height of the hound’s ear, about a quarter of the mark.
  • Keep it at 16 px or larger for the mark, 96 px or wider for the lockup. Below 24 px, use the badge.
  • Use the one-colour version on photographs and busy grounds.

Do not

  • Add a door, an arch or any second symbol beside it.
  • Recolor the eye or the body, or add an outline, shadow, glow or gradient.
  • Stretch, rotate or crop it, or set the name in another typeface.
  • Use an earlier version of the mark.

Color

Ink, paper and one orange.

Flat color only. Orange is a field or a button with ink text. Green, amber and red show status, nothing else. Text meets WCAG AA.

Words

How to describe it.

Citehound is a free scanner that shows whether AI assistants can reach and read a website, with Pro reports, Fix and Beyond for the work that follows.

  • The score measures readiness: crawler access and on-page signals. It does not measure whether any assistant names a brand.
  • Write “Citehound”, one word, capital C.
  • One sentence you can reuse: “Scan, fix and write for the way AI answers.”
