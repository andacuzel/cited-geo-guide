    What agentic commerce means

    A shopping agent is software that finds, evaluates and buys a product on a person's behalf, inside a chat interface rather than a browser tab.

    Instead of returning a list of links, it reads product data directly, picks a match, and can complete the purchase without the person ever visiting your site.

    Agent-driven checkout depends on a discovery step called the Universal Commerce Protocol, or UCP. An agent looks for a UCP endpoint at a fixed location — /.well-known/ucp — not something it has to guess or infer from your homepage.

    If that endpoint exists and describes your catalog, price and availability in a structured, machine-readable format, an agent can act on it directly.

    If it does not, the agent has nothing to act on, regardless of how the site looks to a person.
