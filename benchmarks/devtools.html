Method and limits

What we scanned

48 platforms in this category completed a scan on July 31, 2026. 4 more on the list could not be reached or timed out. They are excluded from every figure on this page.

What we pulled

For each site: robots.txt, sitemap.xml, llms.txt and the homepage, retrieved live. Homepages only. Inner pages were not scanned, and each site was scanned once.

What we scored

One score out of 100 per site, split across three pillars: discoverability (40), technical foundation (20), content & trust (40). The checks and their point values are on the methodology page.

How the sites were chosen

The lists are hand-picked, well-known sites in the category. They are not a random sample, so these figures describe the sites we scanned, not the category as a whole.

Repeat scans

In one October 2026 rescan of the same lists, category averages moved by one point or less. Individual sites moved more: about a third changed by 3 to 15 points.

What this measures

Whether AI systems can reach a site, and whether its homepage carries the signals machines read. It does not measure whether any assistant names a brand in an answer. Model responses vary by prompt, session and training data, and no scan of public files can predict them.

What this doesn’t tell you

  • Anything about pages other than the homepage.
  • Why the unreachable sites could not be scanned, or how they would have scored.
  • Whether any of these signals relates to being named in an AI answer. This page measures inputs, not outcomes.
