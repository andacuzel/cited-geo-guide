Scores are guidance, not guarantees

Your score reflects AI readiness signals we can observe: crawler access, structured data, and on-page trust markers. It does not measure or guarantee confirmed presence in any AI assistant's answers. A perfect score is not a promise of citation.

Scan domains you're authorized to check

Only scan domains you own or are authorized to check. The scanner reads public files, but authorization to check a site is still yours to have.

Fair use

The public interface is for individual, manual scans. Automated or bulk scanning through the interface isn't permitted.
