Common questions

          
What is schema markup?

          
Schema markup is structured data, usually written as JSON-LD, that tells machines what a page is about: an organization, a product, an article, a set of questions. It sits in the page's code and is invisible to visitors. Search engines and AI systems can read it without guessing from the layout.

          
Where does JSON-LD go?

          
Paste the script tag into the head of the page it describes. Organization and WebSite markup belongs on the homepage, Article on the article, Product on the product page, FAQPage on the page that shows the questions, and LocalBusiness on the page for that location. Most site builders have a field for custom head code.

          
Will schema markup get me cited in AI answers?

          
No one can promise that. Schema removes guesswork about what a page is. It does not decide whether an answer engine cites you. Answerable measures whether the markup is present, not what any assistant says about you.

          
What are the bracketed placeholders?

          
Anything the scan could not know, such as your company name, a real price or your opening hours, appears as a bracketed placeholder instead of an invented value. Replace each one before you publish. The counter under the output shows how many are left.
