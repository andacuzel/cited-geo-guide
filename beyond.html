How it works

From their questions to your pages.

  1. Market questions

    We research the questions your customers ask, in their words, starting from your site.

  2. Your pages read against them

    For each question: what your pages answer, what they don’t, what is missing.

  3. We write the content

    New pages, FAQ and comparison blocks, rewrites. Human-edited. You approve every piece.

  4. Publish, then re-measure

    We publish it or hand it over. Each month we run the same questions again and send the citation check, including when nothing changed.

The scope, the number and type of pieces, is agreed in the pilot.

Questions

Before you ask.

What do Fix and Beyond cost?

Fix starts at $299: a fixed quote after your scan, and the price does not change once agreed. It includes 3 months of Pro. Beyond is $490 a month with a 3-month minimum and limited seats, and it includes Pro. These are early-access prices and payments open soon. See the pricing page for what each includes.

Does anything go live without my approval?

No. You approve every piece before we publish it or hand it over.

How do you measure the result?

We run the same questions each month and send the citation check: a dated sample, one run each; API results can differ from the consumer apps. We report what changed, including when nothing did.

Do I need to buy Pro separately?

No. Fix and Beyond both include Citehound Pro.
