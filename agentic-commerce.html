        How an agent shops

        Four things an agent has to do.

        Each one depends on something your store publishes. Here is what, and which Citehound check covers it today.

        1. Find

          The agent has to reach your catalog. It reads robots.txt and your sitemap, and looks for a UCP profile. UCP, the Universal Commerce Protocol, is a file at /.well-known/ucp that lists the services your store offers and where an agent reaches them.

          Checks: AI crawler access, Sitemap declared, UCP endpoint (stores only). How they work

        2. Read

          It needs your product’s name, price, availability and an image as structured data, not only drawn on the page.

          Check: Structured product data, read from one product page (stores only). How it works

        3. Compare

          To weigh your product against others it needs the same fields in a consistent form, and a plain statement of what your brand is and sells.

          Checks: Structured data (JSON-LD), llms.txt present. The commerce panel also reports whether the llms.txt is your own or a platform default. Methodology

        4. Buy

          It needs a path it can follow to complete the purchase, such as the cart and checkout capabilities a UCP profile declares.

          Check: the UCP endpoint confirms a profile exists and declares services. A check that follows a checkout path is in preparation. Methodology

        Questions

        Agentic commerce, briefly.

        What is agentic commerce?

        Agentic commerce is a shopping agent finding, evaluating and completing a purchase on a person’s behalf, inside a chat interface rather than a browser. It depends on machine-readable product data and a checkout path a program can complete, not just a webpage a person can read.

        What is UCP?

        UCP, the Universal Commerce Protocol, is a discovery standard that lets AI shopping agents find a store’s catalog, pricing and availability in a structured format. An agent looks for it at a fixed location, /.well-known/ucp, on a store’s domain. If the endpoint exists and is well-formed, an agent can act on it directly instead of relying on a general-purpose crawl of the storefront.

        How do I make my store readable to AI shopping agents?

        Start with the same foundation that makes any site readable to AI systems: confirm AI crawlers are not blocked in robots.txt, and that price, availability and product identifiers are marked up in structured data, not just rendered visually. Beyond that, a UCP endpoint at /.well-known/ucp gives an agent a direct, structured way to find your catalog instead of inferring it from a general crawl.

        Does Shopify write my llms.txt?

        Shopify serves a default llms.txt for the stores it hosts. In the two we read by hand, the file described the platform’s own commerce instructions and said nothing about the brand. Having the file is not the same as having said anything in it.

        Do the commerce checks change my score out of 100?

        No. The commerce checks are scored separately, out of 8, and are never added to the main 100. They run only when a scan treats your site as a store.
