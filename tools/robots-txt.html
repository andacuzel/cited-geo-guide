Questions

          
Common questions.

        

        

          

            What is an AI crawler?
            
An AI crawler is an automated bot that visits web pages to gather content for a large language model, either to train the model or to answer a live user question. This tool covers the 10 most common AI crawlers, including GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot.

          

          

            Should I block AI crawlers?
            
It depends on your goals. Blocking a crawler keeps your content out of that system's training data and live answers. Allowing it can put your brand in front of users asking AI tools for recommendations. Most sites benefit from allowing crawlers used for live answers and deciding case by case on training-only crawlers.

          

          

            Does blocking GPTBot remove me from ChatGPT?
            
Blocking GPTBot stops OpenAI from using your content to train future models. It does not remove your site from ChatGPT's live answers, which use a separate crawler called ChatGPT-User. To stop ChatGPT from reading your site in real time too, block ChatGPT-User separately.
