Terms

The scan is free. Paid plans have rules too.

Last updated 22 October 2026.

Who we are

Citehound is run by Andaç Üzel, a sole proprietor based in Istanbul, Türkiye. We will update this page when a company is formed. These terms cover the free scanner and tools, the Pro plan, and the Fix and Beyond services. Using any of them means you accept these terms.

The free scanner

The scan and the free tools cost nothing and need no sign-up. Only scan domains you own or are authorized to check. The scanner reads public files, but authorization to check a site is still yours to have.

The public interface is for individual, manual scans. Automated or bulk scanning through the interface isn’t permitted.

Copy-paste snippets the scanner generates for you, such as JSON-LD and robots.txt rules, belong to you. Use them freely, in any project, with no attribution required.

The official Citehound GitHub Action may scan the domain you configure within the stated rate limits.

The API

The Citehound API is for scanning sites you are authorized to check and for generating files. It is the supported way to scan automatically; the public interface is not. A free key allows 10 requests an hour and 40 a day, and the same domain is scanned at most 4 times an hour across all keys. A free key may also scan 5 sites a day. Paid plans have higher limits. These limits can change; the current ones are on the API page.

Keep your key private: you are responsible for requests made with it. Do not use the API to probe private networks, to overload a site, to resell access, to work around a limit or to publish a key. We may revoke a key that is abused, without notice. The API returns the same readiness score as the scan; see “What a score means” below.

Monitoring is part of the Pro, Fix and Beyond plans. A Pro or Fix key can watch up to 5 domains, and a Beyond key up to 15. A scheduled job re-scans each watched domain about every 7 days and we compare each scan with the one before. If you gave an address for alerts, we email you when the score drops, an AI crawler becomes blocked or llms.txt disappears, at most once a day for each domain. Webhooks receive the events you choose; a delivery is retried up to three times, and a webhook that keeps failing is disabled. Only add webhook addresses you control, and keep the secret private. Events and alerts report changes in a readiness score and in crawler access.

The paid plans

Prices are in US dollars. The plans are in early access: payments open soon, and nothing is charged until they do and you agree to a purchase.

  • Pro is $39 a month or $390 a year. The first month is free for the first 10 early-access users. Pro today is a whole-site report of up to 25 pages, a dated citation sample on Claude (ChatGPT is coming), and monitoring through the API for up to 5 domains: a weekly re-scan of each domain’s homepage with email alerts. Features we describe as planned are not part of Pro until they are live.
  • Fix starts at $299. After your scan we send a fixed quote, and the price does not change once you agree to it. Fix includes 3 months of Pro. Where you give us access to your site, we apply the changes by hand. Where you do not, we deliver the files with instructions. For 14 days after delivery we make small corrections.
  • Beyond is $490 a month, with a 3-month minimum and limited seats. It includes researched questions and a gap analysis, 4 edited pieces a month that you approve before they are published, a monthly re-measurement, and Pro.

Early-access prices may change for new customers. If you are already a customer, the price you agreed stays the same for your current term.

Billing and cancellation

We bill through our payment provider. Prices are in US dollars. Taxes, where they apply, are added at checkout. Fix is paid in full when you accept the quote, before work starts.

You can cancel Pro at any time. It stays active until the end of the period you have paid for. You can cancel Beyond after the 3-month minimum with 30 days’ notice. To cancel, write to hey@getcitehound.com.

Refunds

You can ask for a refund of your first payment for Pro within 14 days. Fix and Beyond have their own rules. The details are on the refunds page.

Fix and Beyond work

Before work starts we agree the scope in writing. For Fix that is the list of changes and the quote. For Beyond it is the questions and the content we will write.

You decide what access to give us, and we ask only for what the agreed work needs. Do not paste passwords or access keys into a workspace, a comment or a request form: the workspace has no place to keep them. Tell us how you want to share access and we will agree it in writing.

Back up your site before we change it. That is your responsibility.

In Beyond, nothing is published until you approve it. Where you give us access we publish approved pieces; otherwise we hand them to you. You are responsible for what you approve: that it is accurate, that you have the right to use it, and that it fits your business and the rules that apply to it.

The 14 days of small corrections after a Fix delivery cover problems with the changes we delivered. New work is a new quote.

Acceptable use

Use Citehound lawfully and only on sites you are authorized to check or change. Do not use it to overload a site, to probe a site’s security, or to scan on behalf of someone who has not agreed. We can suspend access that breaks these rules.

What a score means

Your score reflects AI readiness signals we can observe: crawler access, structured data, and on-page trust markers. It is not a measure of whether any assistant names a brand.

A citation sample is a dated sample of what an AI provider’s API returned for a set of questions, one run each. It is not a ranking, and it can differ from what the consumer apps show. Citehound makes no guarantee about citations, traffic, rankings or revenue.

Reports, files and links

The reports and files we deliver to you belong to you. That includes the Pro report, the files from Fix and the pieces from Beyond that you approved. Our research, our methodology and the scanner itself stay ours.

A report or a workspace is opened by a private link, and anyone who has the link can open it. Treat it like a password. If a link gets out, write to us.
