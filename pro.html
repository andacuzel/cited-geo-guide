  2. Ask

What you get

The report, in the order you read it.

Start with the answer.

The report opens with the site-wide score beside the homepage score, then the top three checks to fix first, each with the points it adds and a typical effort. A short summary leads, labeled with what wrote it.

See which problems are one template, not fifty pages.

One row per page, one column per check. A column filled all the way down is likely a shared template: an inference from how many pages fail, not something we observed. In this crawl 4 of 12 page-level checks fail anywhere, and no column fills all the way down.

From the worst page to its fix in two clicks.

Pages are listed worst first. Select one to see the checks it fails, then open its copy-paste fix. Search by path, or filter by a failing check or a section.

Every fix once, with the pages it applies to.

Identical snippets appear once, with the pages they apply to and a Copy button. JSON-LD uses placeholders where a fact is unknown: fill them in, don't publish a placeholder.

Find the questions you're invisible for.

Questions fall into three groups: never named, named only sometimes, named every time. In the sample, 7 of 18 questions never named the brand in any try. Those are the gaps to work on.

Ask five times. Trust the pattern, not one answer.

The same question can get a different answer each time. In the sample, 5 of 18 questions named the brand in some tries and not in others. Across the five tries, the share of questions that named it ranged from 39% to 56%.

Hand it to your developer.

Print or save as PDF opens every section and drops the controls, so the printout is the whole report: summary, pages, checks, fixes and the citation results.

Questions

Pro, briefly.

What do I get with Answerable Pro?

A crawl of up to 50 pages of your site and a report built from it: an executive summary, the whole-site score beside the homepage score, a heat matrix of which pages fail which checks, a page-by-page explorer, every fix once with the pages it applies to, and a printable version. It also includes a citation run on your brand, which we run for you and add to the report: the questions your buyers ask, each asked five times, and the pattern of when your name comes up.

How is this different from the free scan?

The free scan reads one page: the full 16-check report, the crawler matrix, copy-paste fixes and every playbook. Pro reads up to 50 pages and shows what the homepage hides, then adds a citation run on your brand, which we run for you. Comparing two sites is free at /compare, and the citation question sets are free to try in your own assistant through the Answerable MCP server.

What is the sample report?

A real crawl of our own site, answerable-app.vercel.app, run on October 4, 2026: 28 pages. Nothing in it was edited or improved, and our own pages still fail some checks. The report says which. Its Citations tab shows the published citation sample, which belongs to a different brand. Open it.

Does it measure whether AI names me?

Two parts, two questions. The crawl measures readiness: whether crawlers can reach and read your pages. The citation run records whether a model names your brand when it is asked the questions your buyers ask. A readiness score of 100 doesn't guarantee a mention.

Which models does the citation run use?

One today. Every report names the model and the date it was run, and results are shown per model. A result describes one model on one date and says nothing about any other assistant.

Is the citation run self-serve?

No. We run it for you and add it to your report. A self-serve version is still to come.

Is it really a one-time payment?

Yes. Answerable Pro is a single one-time purchase. There is no subscription and no recurring billing.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account or login. Your report is generated and delivered right after payment.
