Privacy

What we fetch, what we store, what we don't.

Last updated 30 July 2026.

What the scanner fetches

When you scan a domain, Answerable fetches four public files from that domain: robots.txt, sitemap.xml, llms.txt, and the homepage. All four are public by definition — nothing behind a login, nothing private. We fetch whatever domain you type in, whether or not it's yours.

What we store

Nothing, today. There are no accounts, no email collection, and no scan history. A scan result exists in your browser for as long as the page stays open.

The one exception: to stop abuse of the scanner, we keep a one-way hash of your IP address paired with a request counter, purely to enforce a rate limit. It expires automatically within a day, isn't linked to which domains were scanned, and is never stored as a raw IP address.

Separately, we count visits from known AI crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and similar) to our own site, so we can publish real data on how often they show up. This is aggregate counts by crawler and by day only — never a per-visit log, and nothing about human visitors is recorded at all.

Will change

This section will be updated when score history ships. Until then, nothing is retained after your browser tab closes.

What we don't collect

No personal data. No email addresses. No accounts. No advertising trackers.

Third parties

Hosting: Vercel. The site and the scan function both run on Vercel's infrastructure.
