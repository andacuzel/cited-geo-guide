Playbooks

Fourteen playbooks. One for your category.

One playbook per category: what has changed in how buyers ask AI, three strategies to act on, and the outdated habits to drop.

A playbook is a short guide for one category. It explains what has changed in how buyers research with AI, gives three strategies to act on, lists the outdated habits to drop, and names the scan checks that apply. Each one can be saved as a PDF.

First step, if you have not done it: run the free scan. The playbooks refer to its checks by name.
