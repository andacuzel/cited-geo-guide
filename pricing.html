Questions

About the prices.

What does early-access pricing mean?

These are the prices for the first customers. Payments are not open yet. Leave your email through the form on a plan to reserve your spot, and we write to you when it opens. Nothing is charged today.

What does each step measure?

The free scan measures readiness: whether assistants can reach and trust your site. Pro adds a citation check: ten buyer-style questions asked to Claude (ChatGPT coming), recording whether your brand is named. A dated sample, one run each; API results can differ from the consumer apps.

Do Fix and Beyond include Pro?

Yes. Fix includes 3 months of Pro. Beyond includes Pro for as long as you stay.

Is the Fix price fixed?

Fix starts at $299. After your scan we send you a fixed quote. Once you agree to it, the price does not change.

How does Beyond work?

Beyond is $490 a month with a 3-month minimum and a limited number of seats. We research the questions your customers ask, analyze the gaps on your pages and write four edited pieces a month. You approve each one before it is published, and we re-measure every month.

When do weekly re-scans and alerts start?

Weekly re-scans with email alerts are part of Pro early access. They are not running yet. We tell the Pro waitlist when they switch on.

Can I do this myself?

Yes. The free generators for schema, robots.txt and llms.txt are there for that. Fix is for when you would rather have it done and verified.

What if I am not happy?

Pro: 14-day refund on your first payment. Fix: we agree the list of fixes and the price first, and rescan to show what changed. The refund terms are on the refunds page.
