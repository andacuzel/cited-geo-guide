What to fix first

    What agentic commerce means

    A shopping agent is software that finds, evaluates and buys a product on a person's behalf, inside a chat interface rather than a browser tab. Instead of returning a list of links, it reads product data directly, picks a match, and can complete the purchase without the person ever visiting your site.

    Agent-driven checkout depends on a discovery step called the Universal Commerce Protocol, or UCP. An agent looks for a UCP endpoint at a fixed location — /.well-known/ucp — on your domain, not something it has to guess or infer from your homepage. If that endpoint exists and describes your catalog, price and availability in a structured, machine-readable format, an agent can act on it directly. If it does not, the agent has nothing to act on, regardless of how the site looks to a person.

    What determines agent-readiness, and why it matters

    Two of the checks below already run in every free scan. The commerce-specific ones — UCP discovery and checkout completability — are what we're building next, so a scan today measures the general foundation an agent needs, not the full agentic-commerce picture yet.

    AI crawler access

    If GPTBot, ClaudeBot or Perplexity's crawlers are blocked in robots.txt, an agent cannot read your catalog at all, regardless of what it contains. Already part of every Answerable scan.

    Structured product data

    Price, availability and product identifiers need to be machine-readable, not just visible in a rendered page a person could read. This overlaps with the structured-data check already in the free scan, though it does not yet verify Product or Offer schema specifically.

    UCP endpoint

    A shopping agent looks for /.well-known/ucp before it looks at your homepage. Without it, there is no structured entry point for an agent to act on — only a page built for a person to read.

    A completable checkout path

    An agent that can find your product but not buy it stops short of the sale. Whether checkout itself can be completed by a program, not just a person clicking through, is what agentic commerce ultimately depends on.
